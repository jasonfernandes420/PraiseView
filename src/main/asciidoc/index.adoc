== Getting Started

=== Starting Your First Service

1. Launch PraiseView
2. A blank new service order will be created
3. Add songs, prayers, and other content from the library tabs at the bottom
4. Use the Up and Down buttons on the left panel to arrange your items
5. Click "Project" to start displaying content on your projection screen

=== Importing Content

PraiseView supports importing songs and prayers from JSON files for quick library setup.

1. Go to **File > Import Songs** or **File > Import Prayers**
2. Select the JSON file containing your content
3. The new items will be added to your library

== Key Concepts

=== Service Items

A service item is any content piece that can be displayed during a worship service:
- 🎵 Songs (with verses and choruses)
- 🙏 Prayers
- 📝 Scripture texts or Informative texts
- 🖼️ Images
- 🎬 Videos
- 🔊 Audio Files
- 📊 PowerPoint Presentations

=== Categories

Organize your hymns by category for quick navigation. Use the category filter in the Songs tab to quickly find songs/hymns. Create custom categories to match your church's organization system.

=== Themes

Apply different visual themes to your projections. Themes can include:
- Custom background colors
- Background images
- Background videos
- Custom fonts and sizes
- Text alignment preferences
- Title display options (all slides or first slide only)

== Advanced Features

=== Live Preview
See exactly what will be projected before displaying it on the main screen. This stage view mirrors the projection in real-time.

=== Service Persistence
Save your services to reuse them for future worship dates. Load previously saved services instantly.

=== Projection Modes
- **Normal** - Display content on projection screen
- **Blackout** - Show black screen during transitions or prayers
- **Clear** - Show only the logo/branding

=== Multi-Monitor Support
Automatically detects secondary monitors and displays projection content across your setup.

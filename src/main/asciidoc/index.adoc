== Welcome to PraiseView

PraiseView is a powerful church presentation software designed to help you manage and display songs, prayers, scriptures, and multimedia content during worship services.

== Main Features

* **Song Management** - Organize and display hymns and worship songs by category
* **Prayer Management** - Store and project prayers during services
* **Multimedia Support** - Display images, videos, and audio
* **PowerPoint Integration** - Project presentations alongside your content
* **Phone Remote Control** - Control presentations from your mobile device
* **Theme System** - Customize the look and feel of your projections
* **Service Planning** - Create and manage service orders for your worship

== Getting Started

=== Starting Your First Service

1. Launch PraiseView
2. A blank new service order will be created
3. Add songs, prayers, and other content from the library tabs at the bottom
4. Use the Up and Down buttons on the left panel to arrange your items
5. Click "Project" to start displaying content on your projection screen

=== Importing Content

PraiseView supports importing songs and prayers from JSON files.

1. Go to **File > Import Songs** or **File > Import Prayers**
2. Select the JSON file containing your content
3. The new items will be added to your library

== Key Concepts

=== Service Items

A service item is any content piece that can be displayed during a worship service:
* Songs (with verses)
* Prayers
* Scripture texts or Informative texts
* Images
* Videos
* Audio Files
* Presentations

=== Categories

Organize your hymns by category. Use the category filter in the Songs tab to quickly find songs/hymns.

=== Themes

Apply different visual themes to your projections. Themes can include background images or videos that appear behind your content.
